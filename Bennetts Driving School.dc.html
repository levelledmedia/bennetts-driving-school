FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit before my first lesson?

Yes, bring a valid learner's permit to every lesson.

What age can I start lessons?

15½ with a permit, no upper age limit.

Do you provide the car for lessons?

Yes, dual-control cars are included at no extra cost.

How many lessons will I need?

Most students are test-ready in 20–30 hours.

Can I book just one trial lesson first?

Yes, single lessons are available with no package required.
