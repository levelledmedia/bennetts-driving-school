Our Services

Driving Lessons

Automatic Driving Lessons

Gear changes and clutch control stress many learners out, and automatic sidesteps both, so you can focus on observation and hazard awareness. Automatic learners often reach test-ready in fewer hours.

Learn More

Manual Driving Lessons

A manual licence lets you drive any car later, automatic or manual, which is why most learners still start here. We build up gradually, from quiet roads to busier ones like Eastern Avenue and Bristol Road.

Learn More

Refresher Driving Lessons

Passed years ago but haven't driven much since? We start with a quick check of where you're at, then focus on whatever worries you most, whether that's the Over roundabout, motorways, or general confidence.

Learn More

Beginner Driving Lessons

Never driven before is exactly where most of our students start. We begin on quiet, low-traffic roads on the edge of Gloucester and build up to roundabouts and junctions at a pace that builds real confidence.

Learn More

Intensive Driving Course

Need a licence fast for a new job or university? An intensive course condenses lessons into a short window, often one or two weeks, so skills build without long gaps to forget what you learned.

Learn More

Pass Plus Course

Pass Plus is a minimum six-hour course covering motorway driving, night driving, and poor-weather conditions the standard test skips, often taken for extra confidence and a possible insurance discount.

Learn More
