What we teach

Driving Lessons in Gloucester

Automatic Driving Lessons

Gear changes and clutch control stress a lot of learners out, and automatic sidesteps both, so you can focus fully on observation, road positioning, and hazard awareness. Many automatic learners reach test-ready in fewer hours.

Learn more

Manual Driving Lessons

A manual licence lets you drive any car later, automatic or manual, which is why most learners still start here. We build up gradually, from quiet roads to busier ones like Eastern Avenue and Bristol Road.

Learn more

Refresher Driving Lessons

Passed years ago but haven’t driven much since? We start with a quick check of where you’re at, then focus on whatever worries you most, whether that’s the Over roundabout, motorways, or general confidence.

Beginner Driving Lessons

Never driven before is exactly where most of our students start. We begin on quiet, low-traffic roads on the edge of Gloucester and build up to roundabouts and junctions at a pace that builds real confidence.

Pass Plus Course

Pass Plus is a minimum six-hour course covering motorway driving, night driving, and poor-weather conditions the standard test doesn’t always cover in depth, often taken for extra confidence and a possible insurance discount.

Learn more

Female Driving Instructor Lessons

Some learners simply feel more at ease with a female instructor, and that’s a completely fair preference. We offer female instructors across Gloucester, trained to the same DVSA standards as the rest of our team.

Evening Driving Lessons

Evening lessons are available across Gloucester at no extra charge, for learners who need to fit driving in after work or school.

Motorway Driving Lessons

With the M5 running past Gloucester at Junction 11 and 12, motorway lessons give you real experience of joining, lane discipline, and higher speeds, all with dual controls and an instructor beside you.

Block Booking Driving Lessons

Booking a block of lessons keeps your progress moving and usually costs less per hour than paying week to week, with an easier regular slot booked in with your instructor.

Proudly Serving Gloucester and Gloucestershire

We teach learners throughout Gloucester, Stroud and the surrounding Gloucestershire area, covering postcode areas GL1, GL2, GL3, GL4 and GL5, and a wide range of local neighbourhoods and villages.

Areas covered include Quedgeley, The Docks, Abbeydale, Abbeymead, Arlingham, Churcham, Churchdown, Elmore, Frampton on Severn, Hardwicke, Hempsted, Highnam, Longford, Longlevens, Longney, Maisemore, Minsterworth, Moreton Valence, Podsmead, Priors Norton, Rudford, Saul, Sandhurst, Slimbridge, Tibberton, Twigworth and Whitminster.

Our coverage also extends through to Stroud and across the wider Gloucestershire countryside, helping learners in both busy urban areas and smaller surrounding locations access local driving tuition. Wherever you’re based locally, we can likely arrange a convenient pick-up and drop-off point.

Bennetts Driving School was founded in Gloucester in 1985 by Michael Bennett. What began with Michael as the company’s only instructor grew into a family-run driving school that now serves learners across the UK, while Gloucester remains our hometown.
