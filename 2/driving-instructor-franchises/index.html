Why Join Bennetts?

We're looking for ADIs and PDIs to join our growing franchise team across the UK. You might be an ADI looking for more work, an instructor who is fed up with your current franchise, or a PDI who wants to join a successful school while you complete your training. Whichever it is, you run your own diary with a dedicated team behind you. If you're not qualified yet, see how to become a driving instructor with us.

Many schools recruit as many instructors as they can without asking whether there is ongoing work for them, which leaves instructors on long, one-sided contracts, paying over the odds and feeling like just a number. We don't work that way. You only get out what you put in, and we want every instructor to be treated fairly.

Bennetts is a family-run and owned school. Kim and Mike Bennett spend their time making sure every instructor is completely satisfied, and a professional phone team books new pupils straight into your online diary. If you're thinking about switching schools, or you'd just like some more information, get in touch for an informal chat with either of us.

Headboard Franchise: Without Our Cars

What we mean by a headboard franchise is that you are providing your own vehicle, either bought, leased or hired. You will still benefit from all the services we provide on a full franchise. The majority of our instructors prefer this method as quite often they join us from another driving school or they may have been trading under their own name so in many cases, they have already sourced their own vehicle.

So if you are looking for a professional straightforward franchise then this may be suited for you. Alternatively if you are looking for a different option please see below as we offer a range of different packages. For more information please feel free to get in contact to discuss in more detail.

Full Driving Instructor Franchise: With a Car

All cars supplied through Bennetts will come fully insured and include all mechanical repairs and servicing. All our vehicles are fitted with dual controls and your vehicle is covered for breakdown with home start and recovery. You can also lease or hire one of our cars without joining the franchise.

So if you are looking for a professional straightforward franchise without the hassle of maintaining your own car and putting wear on it then this may be suited for you. Alternatively if you are looking for a different option please see below as we offer a range of different packages. For more information please feel free to get in contact to discuss in more detail.

Part Time Headboard Franchise

A part time instructor would typically expect to work around 15-20 hours per week, any more than this and we would recommend you to take up a full time franchise with us. Being part time has all the advantages of being with us the same as a full time franchise.

Part Time Franchise: With a Car

All cars supplied through Bennett’s will come fully insured and include all mechanical repairs and servicing. All our vehicles are fitted with dual controls and your vehicle is covered for breakdown with home start and recovery.

Part time maximum mileage per year is 20,000. A part time instructor would typically expect to work approximately 15-20 hours per week any more than this we would recommend you to take up a full time franchise with us.

There is no charge for new pupils and the supply is unlimited. Students are booked into your online diary for you, and are not sent to you as a lead to chase up. The franchise fee is low, with no joining fee, no bond payments and no hidden charges or small print. To get you up and running there are free franchise weeks, and franchise free holiday weeks are available.

You get a website page specific to your area, search engine optimisation for your local areas and advertising on social media, along with incentive discounts for new students. Your top box, livery, appointment and progress cards are supplied free of charge, and branded clothing is provided if you want it.

We've been in your shoes, so we can guide you all the way, with free 24/7 ongoing support and guidance. With over 40 years of knowledge in the industry, we believe we are the best driving school to be with: easy, fair and simple. We are a family run school who care about you.

Our Driving School Franchise

Bennetts driving school is a company with very different principles to the other nationwide driving schools. Our objective isn’t to accumulate as many instructors as possible, nor is it to train as many as we can. Our goal is to be the most effective, suggesting having the right amount of excellent and honourable instructors in the right places where we have a high demand for pupils looking for driving lessons.

If you have been doing this a while, you’ve most likely seen numerous large facilities fluctuate as a result of greed. This is simply not how we work, you can trust we are good for the work and if we can’t assist you, guess what? We will be honest with you at the beginning, how’s that for a change?

We supply ongoing support throughout the franchise process, with a full-time workplace group available to assist you manage and maintain your bookings.

Join a family run driving school that genuinely appreciates your individual needs, progression and career.

We cover most locations across the UK now, however if you are beyond the locations we cover, we generally need a few weeks notice to prepare and gain some exposure in the new area. This permits us time to develop a presence online so that you can hit the ground running.
