About Bennetts Driving School

A family-run driving school, owned by Mike and Kim Bennett and based in Hardwicke, Gloucester. We've been teaching people to drive since 1985.

A Family-Run Driving School with Gloucester as Home

Bennetts is owned and run by Mike and Kim Bennett from Hardwicke, just outside Gloucester.

Mike was a driving instructor for more than 20 years and has trained plenty of new instructors, some of whom still teach with us. We've been teaching people to drive for more than 40 years, and we keep our approach honest, simple and fair.

Today Bennetts has instructors across the UK, from Gloucester and Bristol to Cardiff, Bolton and East London. Wherever you learn, you get the same standards and the same support.

How We Teach

Learning to drive should feel manageable. This is what you can expect from any Bennetts lesson.

  1. 01

    One-to-One Lessons

    Every lesson is just you and your instructor. There's no car sharing.

  2. 02

    Built Around Your Pace

    We start on quieter roads and move on to busier ones as your confidence grows. If you're nervous, we take it slowly and use plenty of encouragement.

  3. 03

    We Come to You

    Lessons can start from your home, work, school or college, or another place that suits you, depending on where you live.

  4. 04

    Checked and Qualified

    All of our instructors are DVSA approved or registered, fully DBS checked and regularly assessed.
