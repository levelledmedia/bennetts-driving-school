How your driving lessons work

  1. 01

    What Happens on a Driving Lesson

    Each lesson is different and develops as you learn new skills and make progress. Lessons mostly consist of driving on local roads. We begin in quiet areas where you can practise before moving on to the main roads. We also cover some of the theory and hazard perception material you need for the computer-based tests, which you have to pass before you can book your practical driving test.

  2. 02

    How Many Lessons Will I Need?

    This depends on your experience, knowledge and driving skills. After a couple of lessons your instructor will be able to give you an estimate of how long it will take you to reach the standard needed to pass.

  3. 03

    Nervous Learners Welcome

    Yes, we welcome nervous drivers. All of our instructors have experience in helping nervous drivers pass their test and, in the end, enjoy driving. We take a calm approach and will never rush you.

  4. 04

    Affordable Lessons

    The quicker you pass your test, the more money you save. That is why we offer discounted first lessons for Hitchin learners, and block booking discounts for anyone who wants to commit to a regular schedule of lessons.
