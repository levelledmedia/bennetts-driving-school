How your driving lessons work

  1. 01

    Starting on Quiet Roads

    Your lessons start on the back roads of Luton, so you can build your confidence somewhere quiet. We focus on steering and basic car and clutch control to help you get a feel for driving, then bring in new challenges as your skills improve.

  2. 02

    Your First Lesson

    Your instructor will ask you to read a vehicle number plate from 20 metres away. If you can’t, we can’t carry on with your lessons. You’ll be asked to do the same on your practical driving test.

  3. 03

    Routes That Build Experience

    Your instructor knows Luton well and plans your lesson routes so you drive through a good range of junctions, roundabouts and pedestrian crossings.

  4. 04

    Manual or Automatic

    Most driving licences are for manual cars. A manual licence lets you drive manual and automatic cars, while an automatic licence only lets you drive automatics.

Learning to Drive in and Around Luton

Before you start lessons you need a valid provisional driving licence. You can apply for your first provisional licence through GOV.UK, and it’s normally issued within a few weeks.

Your instructor will take your training around Luton. You’ll learn independent driving, route planning and driving with a sat nav. To build your rural driving skills you’ll visit nearby places such as East Hyde, Harpenden, Whitwell, Flamstead, Studham, Sandridge, Redbourn, Pitstone, Wigmore, Codicote, Westoning, Toddington, Chalton, Kensworth, Wingrave, Slapton, Flitwick and Hockliffe.

All of our instructors are DVSA registered and know the local area and the local driving test routes. Towards the end of your lessons, your instructor will take you to the test centre area so you can get used to the routes.

When you’re ready to book your test, you can see which test centres GOV.UK lists and where your practical test could be held.
