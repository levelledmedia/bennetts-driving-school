Driving Instructor Dual Control Car Leasing and Hire

Bennetts provide dual control driving instructor car hire and leasing from 2 days up to 24 month periods. You don’t have to be part of our franchise to take advantage of hiring or leasing our cars. They come as standard with dual controls fitted, no vehicle signage and fully maintained and up to date.

There are many benefits of leasing our cars, too many to mention but you won’t have to worry about taxing, insuring or servicing the vehicle or the maintenance costs involved if you did own your own car. You can rest assured that you won’t have any off the road time due to costly repairs or the general things that may go wrong.

We have a wide fleet of cars throughout the UK and our customer feedback is excellent. Most people who decide to use us to lease their car or simply hire for a short period of time are very pleased with how easy and convenient our service is. Our sister site Dual Control Car Hire has more information on our short-term offerings if you wish to check that out.
