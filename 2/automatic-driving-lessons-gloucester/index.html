Learn to Drive in an Automatic in Gloucester

Automatic driving lessons in Gloucester are a good fit if gear changes and clutch control are what stress you out. Take both away and you can put your attention on what the test is really about: observation, road positioning and spotting hazards early. We teach automatic lessons right across Gloucester, for complete beginners and for drivers coming back after a break. If you’re likely to drive an automatic or electric car once you’ve passed, it’s also the simplest way to get there.

We start on quiet roads on the edge of Gloucester and build up from there to roundabouts, junctions and busier roads like Eastern Avenue and Bristol Road. If you haven’t driven for a while, we’ll begin with a quick check of where you’re at, then work on whatever worries you most, whether that’s a busy junction or the Over roundabout. Nearer your test, we’ll spend time around Gloucester Driving Test Centre in Quedgeley, so the roads feel familiar on the day. Whatever your starting point, we go at your pace.

Automatic isn’t the only option. If you’d like to see everything else we teach, from manual lessons to intensive courses and refreshers, have a look at our full range of driving lessons in Gloucester.

Your lessons

How Automatic Lessons Work

  1. 01

    Your First Lesson

    We’ll chat about your experience and what you want from your lessons, then head to a quiet road to get used to the controls: steering, the accelerator and brake, and moving off and stopping smoothly. Complete beginners and returning drivers are both welcome.

  2. 02

    Building Up to Busier Roads

    Once the basics feel natural, we move on to roundabouts, junctions and busier roads like Eastern Avenue and Bristol Road. You set the pace, and we won’t push you onto a road you’re not ready for.

  3. 03

    Getting Ready for Your Test

    Closer to your test, we’ll practise test-style driving and the roads around Gloucester Driving Test Centre in Quedgeley, so you know what to expect. You’ll need to pass your theory test before you can take your practical.

  4. 04

    Automatic or Manual Licence?

    Pass in an automatic and your licence is for automatic cars only, while a manual licence covers both. If you’re likely to drive an automatic or electric car, that’s all you’ll need, and you can always upgrade an automatic licence to a manual one later.

  5. 05

    Switching to Manual Lessons

    You’re not locked in. If your plans change, you can switch to learning in a manual car or change instructor, and we’ll talk through how that affects the number of lessons you’re likely to need.

  6. 06

    Booking Your Lessons

    Automatic lessons are £43 an hour, or £420 for a block of ten, and new pupils can take their first two-hour lesson for £59. In most cases we can get you started within a week or two, depending on instructor availability in your part of Gloucester.
