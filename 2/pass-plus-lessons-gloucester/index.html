Passed Your Test? Now Build Your Confidence

Pass Plus lessons in Gloucester are for drivers who have already passed their test and want more confidence on the roads the test doesn’t always cover. Passing is a big step, but nobody tests you on a wet motorway at night, and that’s often where new drivers feel least sure. Pass Plus is a practical course of at least six hours, with an instructor beside you while you build up to it.

The course covers six areas: driving in town, in all weathers, on rural roads, at night, on dual carriageways and on motorways. Around Gloucester, that can mean building up to the M5 at Junctions 11 and 12 with dual controls under your instructor’s feet, rather than tackling it alone for the first time. We’ll go at your pace and spend longer on whatever you’re least sure about, while making sure all six areas are covered. Each session starts with a quick chat about how your driving is going, so we know where to focus.

Pass Plus is just one of the things we teach. If you’re still learning, or know someone who is, you can see all of our Gloucester lessons in one place. Evening lessons are available at no extra charge, so Pass Plus can fit around work.

The Bennetts difference

Why Gloucester Drivers Choose Our Pass Plus Lessons

DVSA-Registered Instructors

Every instructor is DVSA-approved or DVSA-registered and DBS checked, and lessons are in dual-controlled cars, which matters most the first time you try motorway speeds.

All Six Areas Covered

You’ll cover all six Pass Plus areas, and we’ll spend longer on whichever ones feel least comfortable.

Real Gloucester Roads

Motorway practice on the M5 near Junctions 11 and 12, plus the rural roads and dual carriageways around Gloucester, so you practise on the kind of roads you will actually be driving.

A Possible Insurance Saving

Finishing Pass Plus may help you get a car insurance discount, although it’s up to your insurer. See <a href="https://www.gov.uk/pass-plus">GOV.UK’s Pass Plus guide</a>, then ask them before you book.

The six modules

What Pass Plus Covers

  1. 01

    Driving in Town

    Busy town driving means heavier traffic, tighter junctions and more people around you. We’ll work on staying calm, planning ahead and reading what’s happening around the car.

  2. 02

    Driving in All Weathers

    Rain, fog, ice and low sun each change how a car handles and how far you can see. We’ll cover how to adjust your driving, and the weather on the day shapes the lesson.

  3. 03

    Driving on Rural Roads

    Narrow, winding country roads with few markings are a different challenge to town streets. We’ll practise on the quieter roads out in the Gloucestershire countryside, so you get used to bends you can’t see round and meeting traffic on narrow roads.

  4. 04

    Driving at Night

    Judging speed and distance is harder in the dark. We’ll cover using your lights, dealing with glare from oncoming traffic and staying relaxed on unlit roads.

  5. 05

    Driving on Dual Carriageways

    Higher speeds, faster joining and more lanes to keep track of. We’ll build up to it steadily so it feels like a normal part of driving.

  6. 06

    Driving on Motorways

    Joining, lane discipline and sustained higher speeds, with the M5 close by and an instructor and dual controls beside you, so you’re never on your own the first time. Afterwards we’ll talk through how it went and what to keep practising.
