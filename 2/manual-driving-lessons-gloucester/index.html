Learn to Drive a Manual in Gloucester

Manual driving lessons in Gloucester are where most learners still start, and there’s a good reason for that. Pass your test in a manual and your licence lets you drive any car, automatic or manual, from then on. The clutch and gears do take some getting used to, though, and plenty of learners worry about stalling at junctions. We take it one step at a time, so it stops feeling like a juggling act. If you’ve never driven before, that’s exactly where most of our students start.

We start on quiet roads where you can practise moving off, changing gear and stopping smoothly without any pressure. From there we build up gradually to roundabouts, junctions and busier roads like Eastern Avenue and Bristol Road. If a busy junction or the Over roundabout worries you, we’ll keep working on it until it feels normal, at whatever pace suits you. As your test gets closer, we’ll spend time around Gloucester Driving Test Centre in Quedgeley, so none of it is unfamiliar on the day.

Manual is just one of the ways we teach. To see everything else on offer, from intensive courses to refresher lessons, take a look at the full range of Gloucester driving lessons we offer.

Your lessons

How Manual Lessons Work

  1. 01

    Your First Lesson

    We’ll chat about your experience and what you want from your lessons, then find a quiet road to get to know the controls: clutch, gears, handbrake and steering. We’ll go through moving off and stopping before anything else.

  2. 02

    Clutch Control and Changing Gear

    This is the part that takes practice, so we don’t rush it. We’ll work on finding the biting point, changing gear smoothly and what to do if you stall, so it stops being something you worry about.

  3. 03

    Moving on to Busier Roads

    Once the basics feel natural, we move on to roundabouts, junctions and busier roads like Eastern Avenue and Bristol Road, at a pace that suits you.

  4. 04

    Preparing for Your Test

    Closer to your test, we’ll practise test-style driving around Gloucester Driving Test Centre in Quedgeley so you know what to expect. You’ll need to pass your theory test first, and GOV.UK explains how the theory test works.

  5. 05

    Manual or Automatic?

    Not sure which to choose? A manual licence lets you drive both, but some learners find an automatic easier to get comfortable with. We’ll talk it through at your first lesson, and you can always switch to learn in an automatic if your plans change.

  6. 06

    Booking Your Lessons

    Manual lessons are £40 an hour, or £390 for a block of ten, and if you’re new to us your first two-hour lesson is £59. Most pupils start within a week or two, although it depends which instructors have space in your part of Gloucester.
