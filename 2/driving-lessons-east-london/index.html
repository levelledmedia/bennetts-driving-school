How your driving lessons work

  1. 01

    Starting on Quiet Roads

    Learning to drive in a big city can feel daunting. East London roads can be busy, with buses, cyclists and plenty of traffic lights, and it’s natural to wonder how you’ll cope. We start on quiet roads so you can get used to the controls with confidence, and our learner cars have dual controls so your instructor can step in if needed. As you improve, we build up to busier roads at a pace that suits you.

Learning to Drive in East London

In the UK you need to meet a few requirements before you start lessons. You must be at least 17 to drive a car on the road, and you’ll need a UK provisional driving licence. You can apply for your first provisional licence from 15 years and 9 months old, and it lets you drive a car from your 17th birthday (16 if you want to ride a moped). You can apply online, by post or in person.

All of London is inside the Ultra Low Emission Zone, so if you’re planning to buy your first car once you’ve passed, it’s worth checking that it meets the standards on the Transport for London website before you buy.

Driving in East London means getting used to bus lanes, cycle lanes and busy junctions early on, which is good practice for your test and for driving afterwards. When you’re ready to book your test, you can see which test centres GOV.UK lists and where your practical test could be held.
