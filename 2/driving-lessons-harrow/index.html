Taking Your Driving Test in Harrow

The nearest theory test centres are Watford, Uxbridge and London Bridge (Southwark). There are four DVSA practical test centres not too far away: Pinner is the closest, followed by Greenford, Mill Hill and Hendon. Pinner is seen as the gentler of the four, with fewer big roundabouts and dual carriageways. Greenford, Mill Hill and Hendon have quite a few larger roundabouts and dual carriageways to deal with.

The driving test pass rates for the Pinner test centre are between 49.0% and 52.9%, taken from April 23 to March 24 (DVSA data). When you take your test at Pinner you’ll meet varying road conditions, including residential roads and the busy A4125.
