Why choose Bennetts

Why Choose Bennetts Driving School

Bennetts driving school are a well established and trusted driving school, teaching learners how to drive since 1985. All our driving instructors are very professional, motivated and have excellent knowledge to get you through your driving test first time.

We pride ourselves on our professional driving tuition which is reflected in our pass rates. With a choice of evening lessons, weekends or any time to suit you, our door to door service allows you to fit your lessons around you and your commitments to make it as enjoyable an experience as possible. All of our instructors are DVSA approved or registered and have gone through rigorous training, are regularly assessed and are all fully DBS checked to ensure their quality of tuition is always up to date and of the highest standard.

The Bennetts way

Three things you get with every lesson

Free Use of Online Theory Hazard Perception

Bennetts Driving school offer this service to save you money and time when looking for the material to study for your theory test. Most of our customers find this a very helpful service.

Door to Door Service

With Bennetts Driving School your driving lessons can start from home, work, college or even any central meeting point, the choice is yours.

No Car Sharing

All our driving lessons are based on a one to one tuition basis. We believe you will learn quicker, so we will not pick up or drop off other pupils on driving lessons.

Courses

Choose how you want to learn

Learning with Bennetts is made easy with our fun and exciting approach to driving, whilst still using our great expertise. We provide the following courses:

Weekly Courses

Weekly courses that consist of 1 hour or 2 hours per week, which are paid on each lesson or, if you prefer, paid for in advance.

Condensed Courses

Some people prefer to do condensed courses, where the number of lessons is condensed into several weeks. These are quite popular within school holidays.

Pass Plus

After passing your driving test we find it quite beneficial for young drivers to do our Pass Plus course, which consists of a minimum of 6 hours in all different driving conditions, for example night driving, motorway driving and all weather driving. It helps you get further experience and will also save you a considerable amount of money on your first insurance policy.

For any further details, please get in touch.
